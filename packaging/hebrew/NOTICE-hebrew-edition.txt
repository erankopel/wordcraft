WordCraft Hebrew edition

This is a modified version of WordCraft, based on WordCraft by the ArtCraft team: WordCraft built
with Hebrew as its interface language, open Hebrew fonts, its own desktop id and no self-update.
It is made outside the ArtCraft team, which neither makes nor supports it. WordCraft and the
edition's own changes are licensed under the MIT License or the Apache License, Version 2.0, at
your option (LICENSE-MIT, LICENSE-APACHE, NOTICE). BUILD-INFO.txt names the revisions it was
built from.

Not in this AppImage

  Word's Hebrew fonts (David, Miriam, Frank Ruehl, Narkisim and others) are proprietary. A
  document that asks for one is drawn in the open look-alikes above; the font name in the file
  stays as it is. A copy the person has, put in ~/.config/wordcraft/fonts, is used as is.
  The Hebrew spelling dictionary (from Hspell, AGPL-3.0-or-later) is downloaded only when the
  person asks for it (Install, in the bar above a Hebrew document), with its licence.

Interface text

  The Hebrew interface is storytold/craft-i18n's Hebrew catalog (MIT OR Apache-2.0) with the
  edition's rows from packaging/hebrew/he-supplement.tsv, written for the edition from the plain
  meaning of the English, without another product's translations.
